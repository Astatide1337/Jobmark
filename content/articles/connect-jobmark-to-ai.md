You can use the work you have saved in Jobmark when you ask an AI assistant for help. For example, you might ask for a review summary, a project update, or help preparing for a conversation.

## Before you start

Make sure you can sign in to Jobmark and to the AI assistant you want to use. Only connect tools you trust with your work record.

## Connect Jobmark

1. Open **MCP Connector** in Jobmark.
2. Choose **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, or **Gemini**.
3. Select **Copy link & open**. Jobmark copies the link and opens the right setup page for you.
4. Approve the connection when asked.

That is it. You can now ask the AI assistant questions using the work you have recorded in Jobmark.

## What can I ask?

Try prompts like:

- “Help me turn my recent work into a short weekly update.”
- “What examples from my work would support a promotion conversation?”
- “Summarize the progress I made on my most important project.”

## Revoke access later

Go back to **MCP Connector** in Jobmark. Under **Your connected tools**, choose **Revoke access** next to the plugin you no longer want to use.

This tells Jobmark to stop accepting requests from that plugin and invalidates its Jobmark access. It does **not** remove the Jobmark connector from the AI app itself. To remove the connector completely, open the AI app's connector or plugin settings and delete Jobmark there too.

You can reconnect at any time by choosing the provider in Jobmark and approving the connection again. If an AI app still shows Jobmark after you revoke access, remove the old connector from that app before reconnecting.

## Where do I paste the link?

- **Claude:** Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**. Choose **Add**, then **Custom connector**.
- **ChatGPT:** Open **Settings**, then **Plugins**. Turn on **Developer mode**, then create a plugin.
- **Gemini:** Open **Settings & help**, then **Connected Apps**. Under **Custom apps for Spark**, paste the link and choose **Next**.

If you do not see these settings, the feature may not be included with your account or plan yet.
