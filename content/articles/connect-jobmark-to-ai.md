Your work record is most useful when it is close at hand. Connect Jobmark to an AI assistant you already use, then ask it to help you make sense of the work you have captured.

It can help you shape a review draft, find evidence for a conversation, or prepare an outreach message. You stay in control: Jobmark never sends a message for you.

## Before you start

Make sure you can sign in to Jobmark and to the assistant you want to use. Connect only assistants you trust with your work record.

## Connect Jobmark

1. Open **MCP Connector** in Jobmark.
2. Choose **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, or **Gemini**.
3. Select **Copy link & open**. Jobmark copies the right link and opens the assistant's setup page.
4. Paste the link when the assistant asks for a custom connector, then approve the connection.

That is it. When you ask the assistant about your work, it can use the record you have chosen to keep in Jobmark.

## What can I ask?

Try asking:

- “Turn my recent work into a short weekly update.”
- “Which recorded examples support a promotion conversation?”
- “Help me prepare an outreach message for a colleague.”

Review the result before you share it. The assistant should use recorded facts and should not fill in missing outcomes or shared history.

## Revoke access later

Go back to **MCP Connector** in Jobmark. Under **Your connected tools**, choose **Revoke access** next to the plugin you no longer want to use.

This tells Jobmark to stop accepting requests from that assistant and invalidates its Jobmark access. It does **not** remove the connector from the assistant itself. To remove it completely, open the assistant's connector settings and remove Jobmark there too.

You can reconnect at any time by choosing the provider in Jobmark and approving the connection again. If the assistant still shows Jobmark after you revoke access, remove the old connection there before reconnecting.

## Where do I paste the link?

- **Claude:** Open **Settings**, choose **Connectors**, then **Add custom connector**. Paste the Jobmark link and finish the sign-in.
- **ChatGPT:** Open **Settings**, choose **Apps** (or **Developer mode** if your workspace uses it), then add a custom connector. Availability depends on your plan or workspace.
- **Gemini:** Open Gemini on the web, choose **Settings & help**, then **Connected Apps**. Under **Custom apps for Spark**, add the Jobmark link and choose **Next**. Gemini may limit this to eligible accounts and regions.

If you do not see the setting, the assistant may not offer custom connectors for your account yet. You can still use Jobmark on its own and copy any draft into the assistant when you are ready.
