Keep your notes in one place. Connect an assistant you already use, then ask it to help you review those notes.

It can help you edit a review draft, find examples for a conversation, or prepare a message. You stay in control. Jobmark never sends a message for you.

## Before you start

Make sure you can sign in to Jobmark and to the assistant you want to use. Connect only assistants you trust with your work notes.

## Set it up

1. Open **Connect AI** in Jobmark.
2. Choose **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, or **Gemini**.
3. Select **Copy link & open**. Jobmark copies the connection link and opens the assistant.
4. Paste the link wherever the app asks for a custom connection, then approve access.

After that, the assistant can use the notes you choose when you ask about your work.

To disconnect an assistant, choose **Disconnect** in Connect AI. This stops access from Jobmark; you may also need to remove the saved connection in the assistant’s settings.

## What can I ask?

Try asking:

- “Turn my recent work into a short weekly update.”
- “Which notes support a promotion review?”
- “Help me prepare a message for a colleague.”

Review the result before you share it. The assistant should use recorded facts and should not fill in missing results or shared history.

If you do not see a connection option in your assistant, you can still use Jobmark on its own and copy any draft into the assistant when you are ready.
